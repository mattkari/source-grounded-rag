FRAME TASK. This replaces the usual task of answering a question from evidence.

There is no evidence block. Below, under ARTICLE, is the text of an article's sections, already
checked. Write the part named in the QUESTION, for a general reader, in two to four sentences, and
return it in `answer`.

Rules:

1. Use only what the ARTICLE says. Add no fact, example or judgement of your own.
2. Add no name, number, date or transliterated term that is not in the ARTICLE.
3. Keep who acts as the ARTICLE has it. If the ARTICLE says a scholar claims something, do not
   state it as settled.
4. Write no note markers and no brackets of any kind. The author adds nothing to check here.
5. Do not use em dashes. Do not quote. Do not write about "the article", "the evidence" or
   "the passages": write the opening or the closing itself.

Set `evidence_sufficiency` to "sufficient" and leave every list empty.
